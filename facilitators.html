how we work together

You teach, we handle the rest

Skill Spill exists to make creative learning accessible, welcoming and community-driven. We take care of the logistics so you can focus on sharing what you know.

Skill Spill takes care of

  • Before: finding the venue and date, the workshop listing, registrations, payments, promotion, and all participant emails and reminders
  • On the day: welcoming participants, hosting, supporting you if needed, and photos
  • After: follow-ups, feedback, sharing photos, and your payment summary

We ask from you

  • Designing and leading your workshop
  • A materials list with an estimated cost per person
  • Sourcing materials, if agreed (costs are reimbursed)
  • Telling us about space or equipment needs in advance
  • Arriving 30 minutes early to set up
  • A welcoming, inclusive room

planning

Planning your workshop

Before we announce anything, we agree on the details together, based on your proposal.

Length
We usually recommend 2–3 hours, often a weekday evening (18:00–20:00) or a weekend afternoon.
Group size
A minimum of 5 paid participants for the workshop to go ahead, and a maximum that suits you and the venue.
Ticket price
Usually a student and an employed price, set so it covers your time and the materials.
Materials
What's needed, who provides it, and the cost per person.
Accessibility
Who it's for, any minimum age, experience needed, and venue access.
Date and venue
We find a partner venue and a date that suits you, ideally 2–3 months ahead.

pay and materials

Fair pay, shared success

We use a revenue share, so both of us benefit from a full room.

revenue share

70 / 30

Skill Spill collects all ticket payments. Agreed material costs are reimbursed first. The rest is shared: 70% to you, 30% to Skill Spill for venue coordination, admin, promotion and hosting.

Materials

We agree on materials before registration opens. Depending on the workshop, participants bring their own, Skill Spill buys them, or you buy them and are reimbursed. Please keep your receipts.

invoicing

After the workshop

Within a week, we send you a payment summary: the number of paid participants, total ticket income, material reimbursement, and your share. You invoice us based on it.

Send your invoice within 7 days to skillspillworkshops@gmail.com. We pay within 7–14 days of receiving a complete invoice.

Your invoice should include

  • Your full name or business name, and contact details
  • Invoice number and date
  • Workshop name and date
  • Your share and any agreed material costs, itemised
  • Bank details (IBAN and BIC)

participants

Participants and cancellations

This is what participants agree to when they register, so you know how it works on their side.

Prices
Most workshops have a student and an employed price. Students give their student number when registering.
Payment
Participants pay in advance via MobilePay with a booking reference. Their spot is confirmed once we receive payment.
Minimum
Each workshop needs at least 5 paid participants. If we don't reach it, we decide together whether to reschedule, cancel or go ahead, and participants get a full refund or move to another date.
Cancellations
More than 48 hours before: full refund or transfer. Less than 48 hours before: no refund, but they can transfer their place. No-shows without notice: no refund.
If we cancel
If Skill Spill has to cancel for reasons outside your control, we tell you as early as possible.

on the day

Workshop day checklist

A few things that help the evening run smoothly. We're there to host, so you're never on your own.

  • Arrive 30 minutes early to set up with us
  • Materials ready for the number of participants, plus a few spares
  • A finished example or two to show what we'll make
  • A short intro, then hands-on time as soon as possible
  • Time at the end to look at everyone's work and tidy up together
  • Mention where people can follow your work

promotion

Spreading the word

We promote your workshop on our website, Instagram, Telegram and newsletter. You're encouraged, but never required, to share it with your own community too. Workshops fill best when we both spread the word.

What we need from you

  • A short bio (2–4 sentences)
  • A headshot
  • 2–3 photos of your work or a previous workshop
  • A short description we can use for promotion

You can upload photos with your proposal, or send them later by email.

questions

Facilitator questions

Do I need teaching experience?

No. Many of our facilitators are teaching for the first time. We help you shape the format and we host on the day.

What if not enough people sign up?

We review sign-ups with you two weeks before. If we're under the minimum of 5 paid participants, we decide together whether to reschedule, cancel or go ahead.

Can I run my workshop more than once?

Yes. Workshops that work well often come back, sometimes as a regular series.

Who takes photos, and can I use them?

We take photos for documentation and promotion and share them with you afterwards, so you can use them too.

I have a question that isn't here.

Email skillspillworkshops@gmail.com or message us on Instagram.

our values

Welcoming, inclusive, hands-on, and curiosity over perfection.

We want everyone to feel comfortable learning something new. If you have ideas for future workshops or ways we can improve, we'd love to hear them.
