about

Everyone knows something worth sharing. Skill Spill is where it spills over.

Skill Spill is a relaxed, peer-to-peer space for practical skills, creative hobbies and open knowledge-sharing. Local artists and makers lead the sessions, but the room learns from each other: how someone else threads a needle, sorts their files or fixes a hem.

We keep groups small and the atmosphere easy, so you can ask the obvious question, make a mess, and leave with something real: a new skill, a finished thing, and a few new faces you'll see next time.

faq

Questions & policy

I've never done this before. Can I join?

Yes. Our workshops are made for beginners unless the listing says otherwise. No previous experience needed.

How do I pay?

Pay in advance via MobilePay Box 8051YC and add your booking reference to the message. Paying ahead secures your spot and helps the workshop reach its minimum. If MobilePay isn't an option for you, email skillspillworkshops@gmail.com and we'll make another arrangement.

What's the difference between student and employed prices?

We offer a lower student price to keep workshops accessible. Choose the one that fits your situation when you register.

Are materials included?

Yes, unless the listing says otherwise. Some workshops ask you to bring something, like clothes to mend or your laptop.

Cancellation policy

Plans change, so let us know as soon as you can.

More than 48 hours before: full refund, or transfer your place to someone else.
Less than 48 hours before: the fee is non-refundable, but you can transfer your place. Just tell us their name in advance.
No-shows: we can't offer refunds or transfers for missed workshops without notice. Places are limited, and early notice lets us offer your spot to someone else.

What if a workshop doesn't reach the minimum?

To keep workshops small, personal and financially sustainable, each one needs at least 5 paid participants to go ahead. If we have to cancel or reschedule, you get a full refund or can move your registration to another workshop. We'll always tell you as early as possible.

Should I arrive early?

If you can, arrive a few minutes before the start so everyone can get settled and we can begin together.

How do I contact you?

Email skillspillworkshops@gmail.com, message us on Instagram, or reply to your booking email.

Thank you for supporting small, independent workshops.
